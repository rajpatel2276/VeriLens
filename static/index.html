Why Compare Classical TF-IDF vs. Neural MiniLM?

TF-IDF + Logistic Regression (Lexical)

Converts text into sparse 10,000-dimensional word and bi-gram counts. Runs in ~0.02 ms per claim and provides exact token-level weights, making it ideal for ultra-fast pre-filtering.

all-MiniLM-L6-v2 + Logistic Regression (Semantic)

Maps sentences into a dense 384-dimensional meaning space. Achieves higher balanced Macro-F1 and handles paraphrased claims even when exact keywords change.
